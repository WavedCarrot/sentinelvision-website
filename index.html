How AI Detection Works

Intelligent Visual Recognition in Real Time

SentinelVision uses advanced machine learning models to understand what's happening in your camera feeds—without sending video to the cloud.

What It Detects

  • People: Accurately identifies humans in any pose or orientation.
  • Vehicles: Cars, trucks, buses, motorcycles, bicycles and other traffic.
  • Objects: Bags, backpacks, furniture, electronics and 80+ everyday items.
  • Animals: Dogs, cats, birds and other common animals on your property.
  • Loitering: Flags when someone lingers beyond your configurable time threshold.
  • Weapons (Beta — still in testing): AI trained to detect firearms and threatening objects in real-time.

Local Processing Speed

All AI detection happens on your device in real time. A typical frame is analyzed in 100–200 milliseconds — fast enough to catch activity your naked eye would miss. No cloud latency. No processing delays.

Zone-Based Alerts

Draw custom detection zones directly on your camera feed. You control exactly where SentinelVision should monitor—driveway, storefront, warehouse perimeter, or anywhere else. Objects outside your zones are ignored.

Instant Photo Alerts

The moment a detection occurs, SentinelVision captures a snapshot and sends it directly to your Telegram with details—camera name, detection type, confidence score, and timestamp. You're informed instantly, anywhere in the world.

Analytics & Reporting

Built-in dashboard shows detection trends, people counting per zone, occupancy over time, and historical alerts. Export data for reports or compliance audits.

Fully Tunable

Adjust confidence thresholds, detection sensitivity, alert cooldowns, and schedule armed/disarmed modes per camera. Fine-tune detection to match your specific environment and security needs.

All AI processing is local, private, and secure. Your video never leaves your network.
